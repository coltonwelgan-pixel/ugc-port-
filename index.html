About

Hey,
I'm Colton.

Hey, I'm Colton, a 22-year-old UGC creator based in Vancouver, Canada, specializing in tech and AI/software brands.

I create viral content that shows real use cases for your brand and product in such an organic way that users barely even recognize it as an ad! Content that gets your product in front of the people who actually need it, and gives them a reason to buy today, not "someday."

With a background in sales, I understand the psychology behind what actually gets people to convert, and it's rarely a "BUY NOW." I know how to build desire and trust into a video so the sell feels invisible, not forced.

I test new hooks, angles, and formats on every video because virality isn't luck, it's iteration. My real strength: spotting exactly where a brand's current content isn't landing, whether it's weak hooks, unclear value, or too "ad-like," and fixing it with UGC that feels native and converts.

If your product isn't backed by UGC right now, you're leaving growth on the table.

Let's talk about what your brand's next viral moment looks like. Send me a message and let's create it!
